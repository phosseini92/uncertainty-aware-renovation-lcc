# Implemented scope — v3.2.0 research release

Implemented and integrated:

- uncertainty-aware economic decision core with Monte Carlo futures;
- stable 30/50/60-year lifecycle architecture;
- canonical component replacement-event ledger and RSP boundary state;
- physical BoQ/reference inventory and environmental-factor provenance gates;
- A1–A3 product consequences;
- A4 initial transport;
- A5.1 pre-construction removal;
- A5.2 installation/construction and A5.3 current-construction waste;
- complete B4 replacement event family: product, inbound transport, installation, removal, outbound waste transport, processing and disposal;
- B6 operational energy from explicit physical imports and annual carrier factor schedules;
- terminal C1–C4 accounting from explicit end-of-life assignments;
- D1 material recovery and D2 exported-energy results as separate beyond-boundary modules;
- explicit module applicability, coverage and lifecycle-carbon aggregation gating.

Explicitly deferred / not assessed in the bounded release:

- B1 direct in-use emissions/removals;
- B2 maintenance environmental consequences;
- B3 repair environmental consequences;
- B5 planned refurbishment environmental consequences;
- B7 operational water;
- B8 other user activities;
- A5.4 worker transport;
- environmental-factor uncertainty sampling;
- carbon discounting;
- certified EN 15978 compliance claims or complete multi-impact LCA.

Missing evidence is never interpreted as zero. The default production demonstrator intentionally leaves physical/environmental inventories unpopulated where no source-backed data is available.
